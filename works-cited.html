References & Resources

The following sources are cited throughout this site. Where electronic versions are available, titles link directly to publisher pages or open-access records.

    Dalkir, Kimiz. Knowledge Management in Theory and Practice. 2nd ed., MIT Press, 2011.

    Davenport, Thomas H., and Laurence Prusak. Working Knowledge: How Organizations Manage What They Know. Harvard Business School Press, 1998.

    Matloobtalab, Mehrnaz, and Mexhid Ferati. "User Perspectives and Usability Insights in a Self-Service Portal." International Journal of Web Portals, vol. 15, no. 1, 2025. https://doi.org/10.4018/IJWP.372057.

    Rosenfeld, Louis, Peter Morville, and Jorge Arango. Information Architecture for the Web and Beyond. 4th ed., O'Reilly Media, 2015.

    Ruzza, Mirko, et al. "Designing the Information Architecture of a Complex Website: A Strategy Based on News Content and Faceted Classification." International Journal of Information Management, vol. 37, 2017, pp. 166–176.

    Salvo, Michael J. "Rhetorical Action in Professional Space: Information Architecture as Critical Practice." Journal of Business and Technical Communication, vol. 18, no. 1, 2004, pp. 39–66. JBTC.

Further Reading

The following external resources are recommended for further reading on information architecture, usability, and knowledge management:

  • Nielsen Norman Group: “IA vs. Navigation” — research-based guidance on the distinction between information architecture and navigation design.
  • Knowledge Management Institute: “Information Architecture and KM” — a practitioner-oriented overview of the relationship between information architecture and knowledge management, extending the arguments developed in the Knowledge Codification section of this site.
  • ASIS&T (Association for Information Science and Technology) — the premier academic society for information science research. The scholarship on information architecture, classification, and knowledge organization that underpins this site's argument lives largely within this community.
